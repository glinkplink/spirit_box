## Job post body

We are hiring **four adult performers as four separate fixed-price contracts** (not one person doing four voices).

This is **nontraditional voice recording**, not a commercial, audiobook, or character job.

### What you will record

Short **non-word phonetic sounds only**: individual vowels, consonant textures, very short phonetic transitions, and natural breaths.

You will **not** record:

- dialogue
- character performance
- ghost phrases
- words
- sentences
- names, answers, or scripted lines

Delivery is **neutral and natural**. Your normal speaking voice. No spooky acting.

We will send an exact recording sheet after hire (about **40 raw takes**, plus one short room-tone file). The job is **small**.

### Technical requirements

- **48 kHz / 24-bit / mono PCM WAV**
- **Raw / unprocessed** (no EQ, compression, limiter, noise reduction, de-ess, gate, normalization, or reverb)
- **One WAV per take**
- Treated room or a **genuinely quiet** room
- **Same microphone, distance, and gain** for the whole session
- No phone/laptop mic

### Audition (required before hire)

Send a **~10-second raw** file from the **same mic and room** you will use for the job. We will send the exact audition contents in a message. Do not process it.

### Rights

Paid deliverables must be usable **permanently** inside a **commercial mobile/software app**, including embedding, chopping/editing, filtering, reversing, pitch/time changes, mixing, derivatives, and worldwide app-store distribution. No ongoing royalties. Credit is not required.

**We do not request AI-training rights or voice-cloning rights.** Those are excluded.

A short project-specific rights rider will be part of the contract.

### Budget and contracts

- Fixed price per performer, typically about **$50–$90** depending on room/mic quality and turnaround
- Four **separate** contracts after audition
- Adults only (18+)

### How to apply

1. Confirm you are 18+.
2. Confirm you can deliver 48 kHz / 24-bit mono WAV, unprocessed.
3. Confirm you agree in principle to a project rider covering commercial app embedding/modification, and that **no AI-training or voice-cloning rights** are requested.
4. Wait for the audition request before recording the 10-second sample (or record it if the request is already in this thread).

If you only want to record character lines or processed demos, this is not the right job.
