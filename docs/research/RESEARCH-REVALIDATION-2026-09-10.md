## QUESTION 1 — What happens to a brand-new iOS app in search?

### WHY IT MATTERS
If metadata alone cannot produce competitive rank, the previous commercial thesis is false.

### VERIFIED FACTS
- Apple: search uses **text relevance** (title, subtitle, keywords, primary category) **and user behavior** (downloads, ratings and reviews, “and more”). [Apple Search](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/) [Apple Discoverability](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/discoverability/)
- Apple: promotional text **does not** affect search ranking. [Product page](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/)
- Apple: ratings/reviews appear in search results and **can influence rank**. Same page.
- Apple: SKStoreReviewController may prompt **up to 3 times per 365 days**.
- Practitioners (Reddit iosdev / ASO, 2025–2026): indexing for the **exact name** often takes hours to a few days; competitive generic rank is a separate, slower problem. This is **practitioner evidence**, not Apple policy.
- Apple does **not** publish weights, install thresholds, a 7-day new-app boost, screenshot OCR as a ranking field, or an Apple Ads organic halo.

### INFERENCE
Eligibility/indexing ≠ competitive ranking. A new app can become findable for exact/branded queries and still be invisible on `spirit box`.

### CONTRADICTORY EVIDENCE
ASO blogs assign numeric weights (title 30%, conversion 30%, etc.). Treat as **vendor folklore**.

### UNKNOWN
Time-to-rank, minimum velocity, relative weights, whether Apple Ads helps organic.

### DECISION
Separate **INDEXING**, **RANKING**, **CONVERSION**, **COLD START**. Do not plan as if metadata is sufficient.

### WHAT THIS CHANGED
Launch seeding is required. Kill criterion if search impressions stay economically useless after seeding + iteration.

## QUESTION 7 — Audio architecture

### WHY IT MATTERS
Current experiments do not yet sound like a spirit box. Architecture was locked too early.

### VERIFIED FACTS
- Physical P-SB7/SBox: rapid AM/FM sweep, static, **intermittent speech/music fragments**, forward/reverse, ms dwell. Users **do** report hearing words; skeptics call this pareidolia + radio bleed. [HauntGears SB7](https://hauntgears.com/sb7-spirit-box-review/) [Ghost In My Machine](https://theghostinmymachine.com/2018/11/19/how-does-it-work-spirit-boxes-itc-and-auditory-pareidolia-evp-electronic-voice-phenomenon-phenomena-franks-box/)
- Successful apps: GhostTube VOX = **live streams** (users even report recognizable broadcast like the FitnessGram pacer test); Necrophonic = **phoneme banks** (polarizing: some hear words, others “random / fake / no words”); Bello Ghost Talker = live streams.
- Negative reviews cluster on: **hard paywall**, **repeated phrases**, **no understandable audio**, **too many simultaneous voices**.
- Apple 5.2.3: unauthorized third-party stream catalogs are a **documented rejection pattern**.
- Chopping copyrighted radio/YouTube does not create a license.
- Phase 1 candidate already **is** licensed continuous-speech crops (VCTK 1,200), not isolated phonemes. Renderer density/windowing is the live issue (`SPIRIT-BOX-AUDIO-RENDERER-RESEARCH.md`).

### INFERENCE
We over-optimized **against recognizable fragments**. The desirable level is: **sparse, interrupted, radio-like speech shards**, not sentences answering questions.

### DECISION
**V1 recommended:** Architecture **C/G** — large licensed human-speech corpus (keep VCTK unless the gate fails) fragmented into **~100–250 ms** windows + continuous static + sweep cadence; **allow accidental fragments**; **no** semantic scheduler.  
**Fallback:** Rights-cleared **small** set of talk-radio-like licensed beds (not open internet radio), or commissioned voices if VCTK fails perceptually.  
**Not V1:** live RadioBrowser/internet sweep.

### SMALLEST DISPROOF EXPERIMENT
One 60–90 s A/B on device, unprimed listeners, vs a public P-SB7 reference (do not ship the reference):  
A) current `listening-test` preset  
B) lower vocal occupancy (~5–10% of time) + **longer** 150–250 ms interior speech windows from existing VCTK  
Stop if B is not closer to “radio caught mid-word.” Do **not** commission performers until this test fails for source quality rather than scheduling.

## QUESTION 9 — Economics

### WHY IT MATTERS
A good app with no reachable traffic is a bad business.

### VERIFIED FACTS
Apple proceeds ≈ 70% after year one / 85% in some cases; **use 70%** for conservative scenarios. Prices $1.99 and $9.99 are hypotheses, not measured conversion.

### SCENARIOS (not forecasts)

Assume **70%** proceeds: Tonight ≈ **$1.39**; Lifetime ≈ **$6.99**.

| Target | Tonight Pass purchases / month | Lifetime purchases / month |
|---|---:|---:|
| $1k/mo | ~720 | ~143 |
| $5k/mo | ~3,600 | ~715 |
| $10k/mo | ~7,200 | ~1,430 |

If historical **94** keyword-attributed installs at #1 were **monthly**, even a heroic 20% lifetime conversion is ~19 × $6.99 ≈ **$133/mo** from that keyword alone. That would **fail** the profitability bar unless (a) 94 is not monthly, (b) many other keywords convert, (c) Tonight Pass multiplies, or (d) seeding + rank climb produces far more than the AppTweak snapshot.

### INFERENCE
The old $9K WPPNT story **cannot** be used to say $5–10k/mo is demonstrated for a focused #1 listing. Spirit Talker (different app, $4.99 paid, 700 ratings, stale updates) has **unreliable** third-party revenue models ($200 vs $5.7k vs $10k on different sites).

### DECISION
Commercially plausible **only if** we (1) reach useful `spirit box` impressions, (2) convert on trust/trial, (3) mix Tonight + Lifetime. **Kill** if 90 days after launch+seeding, App Store Search cannot show a path to ≥~$1k/mo at observed conversion.

### UNKNOWN
Our conversion rate. True keyword TAM. Halloween seasonality vs durable demand.
